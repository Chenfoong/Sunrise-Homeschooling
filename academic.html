Frequently Asked Questions

What is IGCSE?

IGCSE (International General Certificate of Secondary Education) is an internationally recognized qualification offered by Cambridge. It is accepted by universities in 140+ countries and serves as a pathway to A-Levels, Foundation, or Diploma programs.

What age groups can enroll?

We accept students aged 7-18. We offer Primary Level (7-11) and Secondary Level (12-18) programs with tailored curricula for each stage.

Is the learning flexible?

Yes! Our flexible English learning modules adapt to each student's pace and learning style. Classes are streamed by ability level, ensuring personalized attention.

What are the assessment methods?

We use continuous assessment, formative quizzes, projects, and summative exams. Students also receive feedback during bi-annual parent-teacher conferences.

Can I transfer credits from other schools?

We evaluate individual cases based on academic records. Please contact our admissions team to discuss your child's specific situation.
