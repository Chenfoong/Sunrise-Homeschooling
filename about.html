About Sunrise Education

Building futures through quality homeschooling since 2014

Our Story

Founded with a Vision

Sunrise Education was established in 2014 by educators who believed that quality education should be accessible, flexible, and personalized. We recognized a gap in the Malaysian homeschooling landscape—families wanted international curriculum standards combined with flexible, student-centered learning.

Our Journey

Over the past 12 years, we have grown from a small learning center to a trusted educational institution serving families across Malaysia. We've maintained our core values while continuously innovating our curriculum and teaching methods. Today, Sunrise Education is recognized as a leader in homeschooling excellence, combining Cambridge IGCSE standards with personalized, flexible learning pathways.
